2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on a visitor's computer, smartphone or other device when they access a website.

They allow websites to remember certain information about a visitor, such as preferences, browsing activity or previous interactions.

Cookies may help a website:

  • Function correctly.
  • Remember user preferences.
  • Understand how visitors navigate its pages.
  • Measure website performance.
  • Improve the overall user experience.

Some cookies are necessary for a website to operate, while others are used for analytics, performance measurement or other purposes.

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

Depending on the technologies installed and enabled on our website, we may use the following categories of cookies.

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for essential website functionality.

They may support functions such as:

  • Website security.
  • Network management.
  • Basic website functionality.
  • Saving cookie consent preferences.

These cookies cannot always be disabled through our website because doing so may affect essential functionality.

3.2 Analytics and Performance Cookies

Analytics cookies may help understand how visitors interact with the website.

At the date of this policy, the website source does not include a Google Analytics tracking tag. If analytics tools are added in future, they must be configured to load only after the visitor has given the required Analytics consent.

3.3 Functional Cookies

The Functional preference controls optional third-party features. On the Contact page, the interactive Google Map is not requested from Google until a visitor allows this category.

The website does not currently use a separate functional personalisation service beyond the cookie-choice storage described under Essential functionality.

3.4 Advertising and Marketing Cookies

Advertising and marketing cookies may be used to measure advertising performance, understand campaign interactions or support relevant advertising.

Siyakha Productions does not represent that advertising or marketing cookies are currently active on the website. If these technologies are introduced in the future, this policy and the cookie preference controls will be updated where necessary.

4. Third-Party Services

The website uses or may connect to the following third-party services. A third-party connection may transmit technical information such as an IP address and browser details, even where the provider does not set a cookie.

Web3Forms and hCaptcha

Web3Forms processes contact-form submissions and hCaptcha provides the form's anti-spam security check. These services are used for the requested contact-form functionality and may process technical and submission information under their own privacy terms. Their form-related scripts are treated as essential to submitting a protected enquiry.

Google Maps

The interactive map on the Contact page is not loaded until a visitor allows the Functional category in Cookie preferences. If this category is not allowed, a local notice is shown instead of requesting the map from Google.

Google Fonts

The website requests the Archivo typeface from Google Fonts. This font request is made to display the site's design and may share technical connection information with Google. It is not an analytics or advertising tracker.

Google Analytics and advertising

No Google Analytics or advertising tracking tag is currently included in the website source. If such tools are introduced, they must be configured to wait for the visitor's relevant consent.

Google Search Console

Google Search Console may be used by the website owner to monitor indexing and search performance. It is a site-owner reporting service and does not itself install a visitor-facing Google Analytics tag.

Website hosting and technical providers

The website hosting provider and other technical service providers may process connection information where necessary to provide, secure and maintain the website. Third-party providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Personal Information and Cookies

Some information collected through cookies and similar technologies may qualify as personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Depending on the technology used, this information may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses.
  • Device and browser information.
  • Website usage information.
  • Online identifiers.
  • Website interaction and navigation data.

Where such information constitutes personal information, it will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data protection requirements.

6. How We Use Cookie Information

Information collected through cookies and similar technologies may be used to:

  • Maintain essential website functionality.
  • Improve website performance and usability.
  • Understand visitor behaviour and website traffic.
  • Identify technical problems.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of website improvements.
  • Monitor online visibility and website performance.

We do not intend to use cookies to collect more information than is reasonably necessary for their stated purposes.

7. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

When you first visit our website, you may be presented with a cookie consent notice that allows you to manage your preferences.

Where the consent system is implemented, visitors may be able to:

  • Accept all cookies.
  • Reject non-essential cookies.
  • Choose specific cookie categories through cookie preferences.
  • Change or withdraw their cookie preferences at a later stage.

Strictly necessary cookies may remain active where they are required for essential website functionality.

The interactive Google Map is held back until Functional consent is given. Analytics and advertising scripts are not currently installed. If optional scripts are introduced later, they must use the consent-controlled loading mechanism before activation.

Visitors may also manage or delete cookies through their internet browser settings.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect how some website features function.

8. How to Disable Cookies Through Your Browser

Most internet browsers allow users to manage cookies through their settings.

Visitors can generally:

  • View cookies stored on their devices.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific websites.
  • Block all cookies.
  • Receive notifications when websites attempt to store cookies.

Browser instructions differ depending on the browser and version being used.

Visitors can consult their browser's official support documentation for instructions on managing cookies.

9. International Data Transfers

Some third-party service providers may process information outside the Republic of South Africa.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, Siyakha Productions will take reasonable steps to ensure that applicable requirements under POPIA are addressed.

Visitors should consult the privacy policies of relevant third-party providers for additional information about their data processing practices.

10. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information processed through our website.

Cookies may remain on a visitor's device for different periods depending on their purpose and configuration.

Some cookies are temporary and expire when a browser session ends. Others may remain on a device until they expire or are deleted by the visitor.

We do not assign universal retention periods to cookies because their duration depends on the specific technologies and services implemented on the website.
