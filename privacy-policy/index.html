Privacy Policy

Siyakha Productions

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Introduction

Siyakha Productions ("we", "us", "our" or "the Company") respects the privacy of visitors to our website and is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and other applicable South African legislation.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or submit an enquiry about our services.

By using our website or voluntarily submitting your personal information through our contact form, you acknowledge that you have been given an opportunity to read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information when you interact with our website:

2.1 Information submitted through our contact form

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone or mobile number (optional).
  • The service or division you are enquiring about.
  • Any additional information you voluntarily include in your message.

2.2 Automatically collected information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected, depending on the website configuration and technologies in use. This may include:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type.
  • Operating system.
  • Pages visited.
  • Website usage and interaction information.
  • Referral sources and general website traffic information.

This information may be collected through analytics technologies and standard website hosting infrastructure.

3. How We Use Your Information

We collect and process personal information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests for information.
  • Communicating with prospective and existing clients.
  • Preparing quotations and proposals.
  • Understanding the services and solutions customers are interested in.
  • Providing information about our technical sound, live staging, printing and branding services.
  • Maintaining business communication and enquiry records.
  • Improving website functionality, performance and user experience.
  • Monitoring website traffic and search performance.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We will not use personal information for purposes unrelated to those for which it was collected, unless permitted by law.

4. Contact Form Submissions and Third-Party Services

Our website uses third-party services to facilitate the operation of our contact form and business communications.

4.1 Web3Forms

We use Web3Forms to process and facilitate the delivery of contact form submissions to our designated business email address.

Information submitted through the contact form may be processed by Web3Forms in accordance with its own privacy and data-processing practices.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy information published by Web3Forms.

4.2 hCaptcha

Our contact form uses hCaptcha as an anti-spam and security measure. When you use the form, your browser connects to hCaptcha and technical, browser and interaction information may be processed to assess whether a submission is automated. hCaptcha is provided by a third party and is subject to its own privacy policy.

4.3 Business Email

Information submitted through our contact form is delivered to our designated business email account so that we can respond to enquiries.

Information contained in these communications may be retained as part of our business records.

4.4 Other third-party service providers

We may use external service providers to operate, maintain and improve our website and business communications.

These providers may process personal information on our behalf where necessary to provide their services. We take reasonable steps to ensure that such processing is conducted in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

5. Website Analytics and Search Performance

At the date of this policy, the website source does not include a Google Analytics tracking tag. Google Analytics should not be treated as active unless it is separately added and configured to respect visitor consent.

The website may be managed through Google Search Console, which provides site owners with search indexing and performance reports. Search Console does not, by itself, install a Google Analytics tracking tag on visitors' browsers.

Google Fonts is loaded to display the website typography. When a page requests these fonts, the browser connects to Google servers and may share technical connection information such as the IP address and browser details.

The interactive Google Map on the Contact page is loaded only after a visitor allows the Functional category in Cookie preferences. If allowed, the browser connects to Google Maps, which may process technical and usage information under Google's own privacy terms.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website uses essential storage to remember cookie choices and uses third-party technologies for contact-form security and delivery. The interactive Google Map is held back until the visitor allows the Functional category.

No Google Analytics or advertising tracking tag is currently included in the website source. If non-essential analytics or advertising technologies are introduced, they must be configured to load only after the relevant consent has been given.

Visitors can change their choices through the Cookie preferences control or manage cookies through their browser settings. Further information is available in our Cookie Policy.

7. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

We may share personal information with:

  • Web3Forms and hCaptcha, to process and protect contact form submissions.
  • Our business email service provider, to receive and manage enquiries.
  • Google Maps, if a visitor allows the interactive map to load.
  • Google Fonts, to deliver the website typeface.
  • Website hosting and technical service providers, where necessary.
  • Search performance service providers used by the website owner.
  • Professional advisers or authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Where third parties process personal information on our behalf, we take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate confidentiality and security arrangements are in place.

8. International Transfers of Information

Certain third-party service providers may process or store information on servers located outside South Africa.

Where personal information is transferred outside South Africa, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that the transfer complies with the applicable requirements of POPIA, including section 72.

9. Retention of Personal Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining business records and complying with applicable legal obligations.

Information may be retained for longer where required by law or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, destroy or de-identify it securely.

10. Protection of Personal Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against:

  • Unauthorised access.
  • Unlawful processing.
  • Accidental loss or destruction.
  • Unauthorised alteration or disclosure.

Access to personal information is restricted to individuals and service providers who require it for legitimate business purposes.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Where a security compromise occurs, we will respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements, including POPIA.

11. Your Rights Under POPIA

Subject to applicable legal requirements, you may have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether we hold your personal information.
  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion or destruction of information where legally permitted.
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Requests concerning personal information can be directed to our business contact email address.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

12. External Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites.

Visitors are encouraged to review the privacy policies of external websites before submitting personal information to them.

13. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children without the appropriate legal authorisation or consent where required.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or amend this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes in our business operations, website functionality, third-party services or applicable legislation.

The latest version will be published on our website with its updated revision date.
